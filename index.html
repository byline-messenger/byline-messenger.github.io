Operational Security by Design

The Problem

March 2025: A journalist was accidentally added to a group chat containing classified US military plans. The information was exposed. A National Security Advisor resigned.

The root cause: Signal's design doesn't enforce operational security. Human error led to exposure, not a failure of cryptography.

Byline is built so that operational security failures become impossible by design.

Why Byline?

🔒 Operational Security by Design

Group actions are logged. Member additions are explicit and auditable. No silent surprises.

🔐 Military-Grade Encryption

Double Ratchet algorithm. End-to-end encryption. Perfect forward secrecy. Zero backdoors.

👁️ Zero Metadata

Server knows nothing. Not even who messaged whom. No logs. No tracking.

🆔 No Phone Number

Cryptographic identity only. Can't be deAnonymized through SIM card requests.

📖 Open Source

Full source code. Reproducible builds. Independent audits. Full transparency.

🚀 DPI Bypass (Coming v1.1)

Works where other messengers are blocked. Masquerades as normal HTTPS traffic.

Who Is This For?

Journalists, activists, and media organizations working in countries with press freedom restrictions.

People who can't afford operational security mistakes.
